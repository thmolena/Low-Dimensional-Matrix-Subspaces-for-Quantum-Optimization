Complement-Robust Sensitivity Subspaces for Repeated Quantum Objectives

The method reserves sensitivity capacity for task directions absent from the development span and provides an exact minimax spectral construction.

Across eight authenticated Gset neighborhoods, rank-four held-out discarded-gradient energy decreases from 0.0590 to 0.0381 on full objectives and from 0.8989 to 0.4396 on localized contrasts. Exact robust action is slower than dense differentiation at this scale.

The paper proves the robust-loss identity and an exact transpose-Jacobian query bound. The package verifies split separation, action--dense agreement, derivative counts, and runtime results.
